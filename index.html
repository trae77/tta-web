FAQ

How much is a website?

Plans start at $129/mo with $0 down (one-page site, hosting, security and monthly edits). Or pay once: $500 for a one-page site, and it's yours from the day you pay.

If I pay upfront, do I pay anything monthly?

No monthly fees from us. The site is yours from day one, deployed to your own free-tier compatible hosting, or pair with our $69/mo Care plan anytime. You renew your own domain (about $10–20 a year), registered in your name. Support is not included with pay once.

How do the plans work?

$0 down, a 6-month minimum, then month-to-month. You own the site after 12 paid months, and there is a 30-day money-back guarantee on the monthly plan fee (see below). After 12 paid months the site is yours and your bill automatically drops to the site-owner rate from month 13: $129 → $69 (Care), $249 → $199, $349 → $279, $449 → $379. Site owner plans (from $69/mo) are month-to-month and have the same 30-day guarantee. The lower monthly rate starts at Month 13, or Day 1 if you already own your site (bring your own or pay-once). An early buyout (after the 6-month minimum) transfers the site and code to you right away, but the lower monthly rate still starts at Month 13. A multi-page site is also available for $1,200 one-time. Pay upfront and the site is yours from day one.

What does the 30-day guarantee cover?

30-day money-back guarantee covering monthly subscription fees only. It excludes third-party domain and carrier fees (such as the $19 carrier registration), and the site license ends on a refund.

When does the site-owner rate start?

The lower monthly rate starts at Month 13, or Day 1 if you already own your site (bring your own or pay-once). An early buyout (after the 6-month minimum) transfers the site and code to you right away, but the lower monthly rate still starts at Month 13.

Can I switch from a monthly plan to owning my site?

Yes. The lower monthly rate starts at Month 13, or Day 1 if you already own your site (bring your own or pay-once). An early buyout (after the 6-month minimum) transfers the site and code to you right away, but the lower monthly rate still starts at Month 13. Upgrading from a one-page to a multi-page plan carries your earned buyout credit over dollar-for-dollar. Care ($69/mo) covers hosting, backups and monthly edits for ANY site you own, one-page or multi-page. The $129/mo Website plan is the one-page build. You can switch to owning the site once the 6-month minimum is done. Your plan sets the site size and its buyout value: the Website plan includes a one-page site ($500 value, each monthly payment credits about $41.67), and Get Found, Never Miss a Lead and Total Growth include a multi-page site ($1,200 value, each payment credits $100). Your buyout is the value minus those credits. Website plan: $250 after 6 payments, $125 after 9, $0 after 12. Get Found, Never Miss a Lead or Total Growth: $600 after 6 payments, $300 after 9, $0 after 12. Once paid, the site is yours that day and the monthly plan ends, with no other fee. Get Found, Never Miss a Lead and Total Growth clients who buy out keep the site but lose the plan tools (booking calendar, ongoing SEO, the AI chat bot and lead responder, review requests, Google Business Profile management, missed-call text-back, campaigns, social posts and back-office tools). To keep them, move to the matching site owner plan (Get Found $199/mo, Never Miss a Lead $279/mo or Total Growth $379/mo). Buying out early ends the plan; if you want plan tools afterwards, move to the matching site owner plan. Without a plan: The site is yours from day one, deployed to your own free-tier compatible hosting, or pair with our $69/mo Care plan anytime. You renew your own domain (about $10–20 a year).
