FAQ

How much is a website?

Plans start at $129/mo with $0 down and setup waived (Starter: a site of up to 3 pages, hosting, security and up to 5 small edit requests/mo, 30 min each). Or pay once: $500 for a one-page site, and it's yours from the day you pay.

If I pay upfront, do I pay anything monthly?

No monthly fees from us. The site is yours from day one, deployed to your own free-tier compatible hosting, or pair with our $69/mo Care plan anytime. You renew your own domain (about $10–20 a year), registered in your name. Support is not included with pay once.

How do the plans work?

After 12 paid months the site is yours and your bill automatically drops to the site-owner rate from month 13: $129 → $69 (Care), $249 → $199, $349 → $279, $449 → $379, All-In $649 → $549 (All-In has a 12-month minimum). An early buyout (after the 6-month minimum) transfers the site and code to you right away, but the lower monthly rate still starts at Month 13. A multi-page site is also available for $1,200 one-time. Pay upfront and the site is yours from day one.

Can I pay for a year upfront?
