FAQ

How much is a website?

Plans start at $129/mo with $0 down (site up to 5 pages, hosting, security and monthly edits). Or pay once: $500 for a one-page site, and it's yours from the day you pay.

If I pay upfront, do I pay anything monthly?

No monthly fees from us. A $500 or $1,200 site comes with free hosting. You just renew your own domain (about $10–20 a year), registered in your name. Care ($69/mo) is optional if you want us to handle security and edits.

How do the plans work?

$0 down, a 6-month minimum, then month-to-month. You own the site after 12 paid months, and there is a 30-day money-back guarantee. Site owner plans (from $69/mo) are month-to-month and also have a 30-day money-back guarantee on the monthly plan fee. A multi-page site is also available for $1,200 one-time. Pay upfront and the site is yours from day one.

Can I switch from a monthly plan to owning my site?

Yes. You can switch to owning the site once the 6-month minimum is done. Each monthly payment credits 1/12 of the site's one-time price (about $41.67 on a $500 one-page site, $100 on a $1,200 multi-page site), and the rest covers hosting, edits and lead tools. Your buyout is the one-time price minus those credits. Example: on the $129/mo Website plan after 6 payments, it's $500 − $250 = $250. After 12 payments it's $0. Once paid, the site is yours that day and the monthly plan ends, with no other fee. Hosting moves to free hosting, you renew your own domain (about $10–20 a year), and Care ($69/mo) is optional.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. You only pay if you want us to build the site.

How do I get the AI lead responder?

It is included in the Never Miss a Lead plan at $349/mo, along with online booking and payments setup.

Do you work outside Colorado?

Yes. We're based in Thornton, CO and work with clients remotely nationwide.
