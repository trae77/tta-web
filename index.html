FAQ

How much is a website?

Plans start at $129/mo with $0 down (one-page site, hosting, security and monthly edits). Or pay once: $500 for a one-page site, and it's yours from the day you pay.

If I pay upfront, do I pay anything monthly?

No monthly fees from us. A $500 or $1,200 site comes with free hosting. You just renew your own domain (about $10–20 a year), registered in your name. Pay once means free hosting only: no edits, no support, no monitoring. Care ($69/mo) is optional: it adds monthly content edits, security updates, uptime monitoring, backups, fix-it help when something breaks, and plan-client prices on all add-ons.

How do the plans work?

$0 down, a 6-month minimum, then month-to-month. You own the site after 12 paid months, and there is a 30-day money-back guarantee. After 12 paid months the site is yours and your bill automatically drops to the site-owner rate from month 13: $129 → $69 (Care), $249 → $199, $349 → $279, $449 → $379. Site owner plans (from $69/mo) are month-to-month and also have a 30-day money-back guarantee on the monthly plan fee. A multi-page site is also available for $1,200 one-time. Pay upfront and the site is yours from day one.

Can I switch from a monthly plan to owning my site?

Yes. You can switch to owning the site once the 6-month minimum is done. Your plan sets the site size and its buyout value: the Website plan includes a one-page site ($500 value, each monthly payment credits about $41.67), and Get Found, Never Miss a Lead and Total Growth include a multi-page site ($1,200 value, each payment credits $100). Your buyout is the value minus those credits. Website plan: $250 after 6 payments, $125 after 9, $0 after 12. Get Found, Never Miss a Lead or Total Growth: $600 after 6 payments, $300 after 9, $0 after 12. Once paid, the site is yours that day and the monthly plan ends, with no other fee. Get Found, Never Miss a Lead and Total Growth clients who buy out keep the site but lose the plan tools (booking calendar, ongoing SEO, the AI chat bot and lead responder, review requests, Google Business Profile management, missed-call text-back, campaigns, social posts and back-office tools). To keep them, move to the matching site owner plan (Get Found $199/mo, Never Miss a Lead $279/mo or Total Growth $379/mo). Without a plan the site is on free hosting only (no edits, support or monitoring). Care ($69/mo) is optional: it adds monthly content edits, security updates, uptime monitoring, backups, fix-it help when something breaks, and plan-client prices on all add-ons. Hosting moves to free hosting (Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages), and you renew your own domain (about $10–20 a year).

What is Total Growth?

Our all-in plan: everything in Never Miss a Lead (including missed-call text-back) plus email and text campaigns, social posts from your job photos (up to 8 a month), and one back-office tool of your choice (quotes, invoices and pay links, or maintenance plan signups). $449/mo with $0 down and a multi-page site (6-month minimum, buyout $600 after 6 payments, $0 after 12, then $379/mo from month 13), or $379/mo if you already own your site. Text-back has a $19 one-time carrier setup fee.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. You only pay if you want us to build the site.

How do I get the AI lead responder?

It is included in Never Miss a Lead ($349/mo) and Total Growth ($449/mo), along with online booking, payments setup and missed-call text-back ($19 one-time carrier setup).

Do you work outside Colorado?

Yes. We're based in Thornton, CO and work with clients remotely nationwide.
