FAQ

Are there any monthly costs if I pay once?

No monthly fees from us. We set up free hosting for your site. The only ongoing cost is renewing your own domain, about $10–20 a year, which is registered in your name. Care ($69/mo) is optional if you want us to handle security and edits.

What if I stop paying monthly?

$0-down plans have a 6-month minimum. If you leave before 12 paid months, you can keep the site by paying its build value minus what you've already paid, or we take it down. After 12 paid months it's yours.

Can I switch later?

Yes. Pay-once clients can add Care, Get Found or Never Miss a Lead any time, month to month. Monthly clients can ask about switching; we'll work out the details with you.

Who owns the domain?

You do. We help you register it in your own name and hand over every login, so you're never locked in.
