FAQ

Are there any monthly costs if I pay once?

No monthly fees from us. The site is yours from day one, deployed to your own free-tier compatible hosting, or pair with our $69/mo Care plan anytime. You renew your own domain (about $10–20 a year), registered in your name. Support is not included with pay once.

What if I stop paying monthly?

$0-down plans have a 6-month minimum, then go month to month. After 12 paid months the site is yours and your bill drops to the site-owner rate ($129 → $69, $249 → $199, $349 → $279, $449 → $379). After the minimum you can buy it out sooner: $250 on Website, $600 on Get Found, Never Miss a Lead or Total Growth after 6 payments ($125 / $300 after 9). If you cancel without buying out or reaching 12 payments, we take the site down.

What is Total Growth?

Our all-in plan: everything in Never Miss a Lead (including missed-call text-back) plus email and text campaigns, social posts from your job photos (up to 8 a month), and one back-office tool of your choice (quotes, invoices and pay links, or maintenance plan signups). $449/mo with $0 down and a multi-page site (6-month minimum, buyout $600 after 6 payments, $0 after 12, then $379/mo from month 13), or $379/mo if you already own your site. Text-back has a $19 one-time carrier setup fee.

What does the 30-day guarantee cover?

30-day money-back guarantee covering monthly subscription fees only. It excludes third-party domain and carrier fees (such as the $19 carrier registration), and the site license ends on a refund.

When does the site-owner rate start?

The lower monthly rate starts at Month 13, or Day 1 if you already own your site (bring your own or pay-once). An early buyout (after the 6-month minimum) transfers the site and code to you right away, but the lower monthly rate still starts at Month 13.

Can I switch later?

Yes. Upgrading from a one-page to a multi-page plan carries your earned buyout credit over dollar-for-dollar. Care ($69/mo) covers hosting, backups and monthly edits for ANY site you own, one-page or multi-page. The $129/mo Website plan is the one-page build. The lower monthly rate starts at Month 13, or Day 1 if you already own your site (bring your own or pay-once). An early buyout (after the 6-month minimum) transfers the site and code to you right away, but the lower monthly rate still starts at Month 13. Pay-once clients can add Care, Get Found, Never Miss a Lead or Total Growth any time, month to month. Monthly clients can switch to owning the site once the 6-month minimum is done. Your plan sets the site size and its buyout value: the Website plan includes a one-page site ($500 value, each monthly payment credits about $41.67), and Get Found, Never Miss a Lead and Total Growth include a multi-page site ($1,200 value, each payment credits $100). Your buyout is the value minus those credits. Website plan: $250 after 6 payments, $125 after 9, $0 after 12. Get Found, Never Miss a Lead or Total Growth: $600 after 6 payments, $300 after 9, $0 after 12. Get Found, Never Miss a Lead and Total Growth clients who buy out keep the site but lose the plan tools (booking calendar, ongoing SEO, the AI chat bot and lead responder, review requests, Google Business Profile management, missed-call text-back, campaigns, social posts and back-office tools). To keep them, move to the matching site owner plan (Get Found $199/mo, Never Miss a Lead $279/mo or Total Growth $379/mo). After the 6-month minimum, you can pay a buyout balance to own your site immediately ($250 for 1-page / $600 for multi-page at month 6, scaling down monthly). Paying an early buyout transfers full site ownership to you right away; your monthly rate drops to the lower Month 13+ tier starting on your 13th billing cycle. Alternatively, paying for a full year upfront via annual pre-pay counts as all 12 payments—giving you immediate site ownership and unlocking the lower rate starting in Year 2. Without a plan: The site is yours from day one, deployed to your own free-tier compatible hosting, or pair with our $69/mo Care plan anytime. You renew your own domain (about $10–20 a year).
