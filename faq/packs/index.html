Questions

How much does a website cost?

Path 1 — Pay once. Yours from day one. No monthly fees from us. Free hosting. You renew your own domain (about $10–20/yr). Care $69/mo is optional.

  • One-page site: $500 once. Buy now
  • Multi-page site: $1,200 once. Buy now

Path 2 — $0 down. Monthly plans. You own the site after 12 paid months; the bill drops to the site-owner rate from month 13. 6-month minimum (All-In: 12), then month to month.

  • All-In: $649/mo → $549/mo from month 13 (12-month minimum). Start All-In
  • Starter: $129/mo → Care $69/mo from month 13. Start Starter
  • Get Found: $249/mo → $199/mo from month 13. Start Get Found
  • Never Miss a Lead: $349/mo → $279/mo from month 13. Start Never Miss a Lead
  • Total Growth: $449/mo → $379/mo from month 13. Start Total Growth
Which plan is right for me?
  • All-In — you want everything done for you.
  • Starter / Care — clean one-page storefront and basic support.
  • Get Found — you’re hard to find on Google Maps and AI search.
  • Never Miss a Lead — you miss calls and need booking, chat, and text-back.
  • Total Growth — you also want email/SMS, social posts, and a back-office tool.
What is Care ($69/mo)?

Optional on Path 1; included after Starter’s month-13 step-down. Up to 5 small content edits a month (about 30 minutes each; unused edits don’t roll over), security updates, uptime monitoring, backups, fix-it help when something breaks, and member prices on add-ons (30% off, setup fees waived).

What’s the money-back guarantee?

30-day money-back on monthly subscription fees only. It doesn’t cover third-party domain fees. On a refund, the site license ends.

Can I buy out early on a monthly plan?

After the 6-month minimum, yes. Starter ($0-down) is a one-page site: buyout about $250 after 6 months, $125 after 9, $0 after 12. Get Found / Never Miss a Lead / Total Growth are multi-page: about $600 after 6, $300 after 9, $0 after 12. Early buyout transfers the site and code right away; the lower monthly rate still starts at month 13. After buyout, plan tools continue only while you keep paying that plan (or a site-owner plan). Without Care, pay-once / bought-out sites sit on free hosting only (no edits, support, or monitoring).

Who registers the domain?

You do, in your own name, at registrar cost (about $10–20/yr). The domain is never free. We help point it at the site.

Referral credit?

Refer another local business owner who signs up for a monthly plan and gets their first bill paid — you get one free month of your current plan fee (Stripe customer balance credit).

What is an industry pack?

Industry packs are optional monthly add-ons for plan members. Trades Pack is $79/mo; Restaurant & Bar Pack is $59/mo. Max 2 packs per plan. Setup included; 6-month minimum. SEO, GBP, review requests, booking, payments, and the AI chatbot stay inside plans — not sold inside packs.

Do packs replace a plan?

No. You need a monthly plan (or All-In, which includes one pack of choice). Packs add industry pages/tools on top.
