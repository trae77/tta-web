What is All-In?

Our top plan: everything in Total Growth plus the Private AI Assistant, every custom page, both back-office tools, 5 city pages, a Spanish version, a conversion audit, an accessibility check, a logo kit, a newsletter, an industry pack of your choice and new tools as they launch. $649/mo, then $549/mo from month 13, 12-month minimum. Or prepay $6,620 for the year.

Which plan do most businesses pick?

Never Miss a Lead ($349/mo): the site, Google setup, missed-call text-back, the AI chatbot, review requests and booking. If you mostly want more calls, Get Found ($249/mo) covers a multi-page site, your Google Business Profile, local SEO and AI search setup. Smaller budget? Starter is $129/mo.

Do I own the site?

Yes. On Path 1 it's yours from day one. On Path 2 it's yours after 12 payments, and your bill drops to the owner rate from month 13.

Is there a contract or minimum?

Path 2 plans have a 6-month minimum (All-In: 12 months), then they're month to month. After the minimum you can buy the site out early. There's a 30-day money-back guarantee on the plan fee.

Can I pay for the year upfront?

Yes, and you save at least 15%: Starter $1,315, Get Found $2,539, Never Miss a Lead $3,559, Total Growth $4,579, All-In $6,620. It counts as all 12 payments, so the site is yours right away.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Every plan is $0 down with setup waived.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. Keep it until the new one beats it; we build a free mockup first so you can compare. If you'd rather keep owning your own site, site-owner Care starts at $69/mo.

I'm locked into a contract with someone else.

No problem. Tell us when it ends and we'll check back 30 days before, so the new site is ready the day you're free.

How do add-ons work?

Add-ons work with either path. Plan members (and pay-once owners with Care) get 30% off add-ons with setup waived. See add-ons

Do you need my passwords?

No. Never send passwords. We'll ask you to add us as a user or manager on your accounts.

Who registers the domain?

You do, in your own name, at registrar cost (about $10–20/yr). We help set it up.
